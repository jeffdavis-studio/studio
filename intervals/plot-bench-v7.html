Tint is drawn per anchor at setup, so moving these re-runs setup(). The lightness-gap rejection loops read lightness, which is what tint moves — so a different range can re-roll an anchor's inks, not just lighten them. That is the artwork, not a bug.
Composition & plot
The composition is 355.6 × 279.4 mm — 14 × 11 in LANDSCAPE, on a 14 × 17 sheet with 1.5 in margins. It is turned a quarter turn clockwise into the document, which is fixed at 297 × 410 mm: the plotter's working area, not the paper (plot-frame.mjs). The document is not a control and never should be. Changing the composition here does not change the sheet or the margins — those are physical.
Export is the artwork's own exportPlotFiles() — the same call key “p” makes on Intervals_v7.html. The bench adds no geometry of its own, so a file exported here is the file the artwork writes, at the settings shown.
