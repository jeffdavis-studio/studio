Registration marks — backing sheet

Plot this once, onto a scrap sheet taped to the board. Not onto art paper. Single pen, one lift per mark, under a minute. Then rule the paper rectangle off the centerlines and tape a card L‑bracket at the top‑right corner — home is upper‑right — and every 14 × 17 sheet after that drops into the same place.

Paper overhang: 29.3 mm each side, 10.9 mm top and bottom — the sheet is bigger than the window, which is why its outline cannot be plotted. Image slack at 11 in: 8.8 mm each side, 65.3 mm top and bottom.
