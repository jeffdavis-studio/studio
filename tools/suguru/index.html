Puzzles

How it works

Fill every cage (the heavy-outlined blocks) with the digits 1 to N, where N is how many cells the cage has. A 3-cell cage gets 1, 2, 3 — in any order.
  • No two touching cells may hold the same digit — including diagonally.
  • Every puzzle here has exactly one solution and can be reached by pure logic. No guessing, ever.
Tap a cell, then tap a digit. Notes writes small pencil marks instead. Conflicts light up red as you go. Progress saves on this device.
