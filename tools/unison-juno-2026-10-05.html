Unison — the Juno

The workhorse, synthesised live in your browser from src/instruments/juno.js — the same file the song render calls. Nothing here is a sample. The patch book: every archetype a song can draw, grouped by the part it plays. Each one opens at its base setting; Draw a variation moves its sliders inside the range a song draws from, the way a song does.

Tap the keys, or type A W S E D F T G Y H U J K, Z / X for octaves. Touch sets how hard: a hard note is louder and opens the filter further. Chords, phrases and the progression go through the voice's own schedule(), the entry point the song uses; tap a playing one again to stop it.
