Key Findings
OCMT is the strongest gateway. 71% of collectors who entered through On Chain Memory Token went on to collect additional projects — more than double the next best (Inflection at 35%).
399 wallets hold half the supply. 18.6% of collectors own 2+ projects and hold 49% of all tokens. The 32 deepest collectors (6+ projects) alone hold 15%.
DfaM is acquisition, not yet retention. 672 collectors — the largest base — but 0% have crossed into other projects. Expected for the newest large edition, but the conversion pipeline starts here.
OCMT↔Progression is the strongest pair (90 shared wallets). The recent projects cluster tightly. Earlier projects (Construction Token, Rhythm) share collectors more broadly.
