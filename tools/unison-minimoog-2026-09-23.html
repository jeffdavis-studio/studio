Unison — the Minimoog

The mono bass voice, synthesized live in your browser from src/instruments/minimoog.js, the same file the song render calls. Nothing here is a sample.

One voice, last-note priority, the way the Model D plays: hold a key and press another and the pitch glides there without re-striking the contours; let go first and the next note strikes fresh. Click the keys or type A W S E D F T G Y H U J K, Z / X to shift octaves. Bass line plays a two-bar line through the voice's own schedule(), the entry point the song uses.

What was measured, what moved, and what the reference pack could not tell us

The reference pack is seven recordings of a real Minimoog (freesound, CC0 / CC-BY), each checked by measurement before use. It confirmed the circuit: the filter falls faster than any 12 dB filter can, so it is a 24 dB ladder, and the release darkens with the decay switch on, as ours does. One real recording's four-second filter sweep is traced by this model to within 1.5 dB/oct.

Moved, on the fat patch only: VCO 3 (one octave down) sits 12.6 dB under the note, as in a real Minimoog open bass (level 0.55 → 0.23), and VCO 2 is detuned 1 cent rather than 7, because a real "deep detuned" Minimoog bass beats about a quarter as hard as 7 cents does.
