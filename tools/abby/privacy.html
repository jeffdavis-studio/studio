Abby privacy policy

Abby is a private assistant application used by a single person on a single computer they control. It accesses Google services only for the account of its owner, who installs and authorizes it.

  • Data from Google services is read and written only on the owner's computer and is not transmitted to any third party.
  • No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The application has no users other than its owner and collects nothing from anyone else.

Last updated October 26, 2026.
