What was measured, what moved, and what the reference pack could not tell us

The reference pack is recordings of real electric basses from freesound (CC0 and CC-BY): one player's short-scale bass recorded straight in, eight notes deep from soft to hard, low E up to the D an octave and a half above; a picked note on another bass; and a long open E on a P-style bass left to ring for sixteen seconds. Every file was checked by measurement before use. One, sold as a slap note, turned out not to be a single clean string and was thrown out. On 10/05 the pack grew for the instruments: a P-style bass straight in, a J-style, a fretless with its bridge and neck pickups each soloed (open strings only), an active bass with an onboard preamp, and a natural harmonic. Nothing clean turned up for a stopped fretless note, a slide or a slap, so those are built from how they are played.

Moved, from the real bass: how stiff the strings are (the overtones run slightly sharp, more so up the neck), how long a note rings and how fast its upper overtones die away, how bright the attack is and how much brighter it gets when you dig in, and the slow two-speed fade of a long note. The biggest single change was the tone: a real bass straight into the desk is much darker in its body than my first build, so the electronics now work the way a passive bass's do, a tone control on top of the pickup's own resonance, and the tone sits well back.

Still off, on the record for the next pass: the open low strings are brighter in their body than the real one, and nothing on the panel closes that, so it is the model and not a setting. The picked attack is a little softer than the real picked note.

Voiced, not measured: slap and palm-muted. No clean recording of either on a real bass turned up, so these are built from how the instrument is played, not fitted.

The book (instruments and styles), 2026-10-05. Fitted means set by measurement against a real recording of that instrument; voiced means built from how the instrument is made and played, because no clean recording turned up.
