Unison — the Juno

The poly voice, synthesised live in your browser from src/instruments/juno.js — the same file the song render calls. Nothing here is a sample.

Click the keys, or use your keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K for one octave, Z / X to shift octaves. The chord buttons go through the voice's own schedule(), which is the same entry point the song uses — a poly synth judged one note at a time is not judged at all.
