Unison for the flight

Updated Monday, November 16. For your phone, with headphones.

On the plane. Each page is a small download, about a third of a megabyte, and after it loads it needs no connection: the sound is made on your phone. Open the ones you want before you board, or over the in-flight Wi-Fi. Your marks are saved on the phone as you go. When you finish a page, or stop partway, press Copy link and text it to Morgan whenever you have a signal. If you hear nothing, check the silent switch on the side of the phone.

1. Effects by ear

  • Effects audition
    Pick a song and a part, press play, and switch one effect on at a time against dry. Mark each keep, cut or unsure. Your keeps become the menu.

2. Ear review: drums, guitar, bass

  • Drum kits
    Every kit plays the same four-bar groove, then each drum alone.
  • Rhythm guitar
    Every pedal and amp sound on one fixed part, then every playing style.
  • Bass guitar
    Same layout as guitar.

3. Patch curation: the four synths

  • FM
    Start here. About half an hour.
  • OB-Xa
  • Juno
  • Minimoog
    No chord row, since it plays one note at a time.

4. The jam, if you want to just listen

  • Jam page
    Since November 15 the parts move around the root and there are four chord voicings. If any of it is too much, note which to turn down: bass, arp, lead, or the chord voicing.

Eight review pages and the jam. No order is required.
