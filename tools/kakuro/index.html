How it works

Fill the white cells with digits 1–9. Each clue cell gives the total for the run of white cells beside it: the number in the upper right is the sum going across, the number in the lower left is the sum going down.
  • A digit may not repeat inside a single run.
  • Every puzzle here has exactly one solution and can be reached by pure logic. No guessing, ever — that's the whole point of the set.
Tap a cell, then tap a digit; the bar above the keypad shows both of that cell's clues and what's left to make. Notes writes pencil marks. Check flags any digit that disagrees with the solution. Zoom enlarges the grid — it scrolls. Progress saves on this device.
